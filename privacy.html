privacy policy

effective november 13, 2026

This policy covers the websites, tools and applications operated by Ency (Noah Chun, New York, NY), including apps that ask you to sign in with Google ("the Services"). Questions: noah@ency.world.

What we collect

  • Information you give us directly, such as your name, email address or phone number when you sign up, contact us or join a list.
  • If you sign in with Google and grant access, the Google account data covered by the permissions you approve on the consent screen. Depending on the feature, this can include your basic profile (name, email, profile picture) and content from Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs or Sheets.
  • Basic technical data our hosting providers log automatically, such as IP address, browser type and request times, used for security and to keep the Services running.

How we use Google user data

Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features you asked for, for example reading, organizing, drafting or sending email, managing calendar events, or reading and writing documents and spreadsheets on your behalf. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Ency's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Humans do not read your Google data unless you give explicit permission for specific messages or files, it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, to comply with applicable law, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized for internal operations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers that help run the Services (such as hosting and AI processing providers), under confidentiality obligations and only as needed to perform the feature you requested, or when required by law, or with your consent.

Storage and security

Data is stored with reputable cloud providers and encrypted in transit. OAuth tokens are stored securely and used only to make the requests you have authorized. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Retention and deletion

We keep data only as long as needed to provide the Services. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To have data we hold about you deleted, email noah@ency.world and we will delete it within 30 days, except where we must keep it by law.

Children

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version. Material changes affecting Google user data will be communicated before they take effect.
